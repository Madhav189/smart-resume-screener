Job Title: Backend Software Engineer
Department: Engineering

We are looking for a Backend Software Engineer to join our core infrastructure team. You will be responsible for building scalable distributed backend systems.

Requirements:
- 2+ years of backend development experience.
- Strong proficiency in Java and Python.
- Experience building REST APIs.
- Solid understanding of SQL and relational databases (PostgreSQL preferred).
- Familiarity with in-memory data stores like Redis.
- Experience deploying applications with Docker and AWS.

Nice to have:
- Experience with Spring Boot.
- Familiarity with Kubernetes.
- Bachelor's degree in Computer Science.

You should have excellent problem-solving skills and be able to communicate effectively within a team environment.
